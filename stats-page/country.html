Definitions and method

The Apple review storefront a show draws most of its reviews from decides, because an audience reviews from where it listens. That alone would misplace a show that travels well, so it is gated on concentration: when fewer than 70% of a show's reviews come from its top storefront, the country of the show's own host decides instead. Each show lands in exactly one country, so no show is counted twice across country pages.

Show counts, episode counts, ratings, review counts, platform presence and sponsor flags are observed. Monthly listener figures and audience composition are modeled estimates, marked with a badge, and are best read as directional.

Measured from each show's most recent episode date against the snapshot date. Active means a release inside the stated window. It is not a guarantee of a fixed schedule, and a show can be counted active on a single episode.

State or territory and city come from the show's host record, and only where that host is in Australia. A show can belong to this country on storefront evidence while its host lives elsewhere, and that host's location says nothing about where the show sits here. Shares are computed on the shows where the field is known, and that base is printed above each table.

The sum of each show's modeled monthly listeners, so it counts listener-show pairs rather than unique people. One person following six Australian shows appears six times. The unique Australian audience is far smaller.

Observable brand activity detected at some point in a show's run. Undetected sponsorship exists, so the figure is a floor. It says nothing about deal size, and it is not evidence of current revenue.

The launch chart counts shows still in the catalog today, by their first year of activity. Shows that launched and were removed are absent, so early years read lower than they were at the time.

Listener-weighted modeled estimates, so a show with 100,000 listeners moves the figure a hundred times more than a show with 1,000. Shows with no estimate are excluded rather than counted as zero.
