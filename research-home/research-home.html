How we work

MillionPodcasts maintains a continuously updated database of 2.7 million podcasts, spanning show metadata, episode catalogues, host and contributor records, distribution platforms, sponsor activity, audience signals and engagement metrics. Our research team draws on this catalogue to produce reports on the questions shaping the podcast industry, each built on a defined sample, a documented methodology and a clearly stated snapshot date. Where figures are modelled rather than directly observed, such as listener estimates and audience demographics, we say so and explain how the model works; where the data has limits, we name them.
